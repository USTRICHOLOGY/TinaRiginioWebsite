For nearly 30 years, I mastered the hows of hair: cutting, coloring, styling, extensions and everything in between. Eventually I realized there was something far more important I wanted to understand. The whys.

Why were so many men and women experiencing hair loss? Why did it seem to be happening more often? And what could I do to make a meaningful difference?

My connection to hair loss is deeply personal. My mother battled cancer throughout my life, and I knew her without hair for more years than I knew her with it. Watching her journey, I saw the emotional impact hair loss has on a person's confidence, identity and well-being.

I could transform someone's appearance behind the salon chair, but I wanted to do more: understand the underlying factors, provide education, and help people through a journey that can feel overwhelming and isolating. That passion led me to become a Certified Clinical Trichologist.

Today I combine nearly three decades of hands-on experience with specialized education in hair and scalp health. Whether you're dealing with gradual thinning, excessive shedding, alopecia, hormonal changes, or hair loss connected to illness or medical treatment, my approach begins with listening, understanding and education. There is no one-size-fits-all answer, and you'll feel supported every step of the way.

MY MISSION

No one should navigate hair loss feeling alone.

To help people understand the why behind their hair loss, find a path forward, and regain a sense of confidence and control.
